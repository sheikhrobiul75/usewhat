About UseWhat

UseWhat is a practical idea-discovery platform built around a simple question: What can I do with what I already have?

Instead of searching the internet for hours, users can enter an item they already have and discover useful ways to make, repair, reuse, save money, or create value.

The long-term goal is to build a large database of practical ideas that helps people use their existing resources more intelligently.
